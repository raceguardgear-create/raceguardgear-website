Who We Are

One Supplier,
Three Ways We Help.

Race Guard Gear works across competitive motorsport and everyday riding — supplying parts, renting race-ready kit, and equipping riders with protective gear.

Our Focus

A supplier that understands
the build, not just the order.

We work with student motorsport teams, karting outfits and independent racers who need gear and parts that hold up under real competition conditions — not just off a shelf.

Alongside that, we supply protective riding gear for everyday riders, and rent out race-ready kit for teams and individuals who need it for a single event rather than a full purchase.

Beyond supply, we offer technical sourcing and custom fabrication support, so teams building a car from scratch have a partner for both the driver's kit and the machine itself.
